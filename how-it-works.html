How it works

A route, not just a course

Affinity is a structured development environment. You build foundations fast, deepen them over a year, and stay inside a rhythm of live coaching and accountability until readiness stops being a question.

The journey

Three stages, one standard

90-day bootcamp

Intensive preparation before you enter the market. Confidence, communication and sales fundamentals, practised until they hold under pressure.

12-month curriculum

The longer runway. A rigorous sales development curriculum built for lasting commercial capability, not a quick certificate.

Operational readiness

Trained, prepared and market-ready before placement, so employers absorb less foundation work and you arrive already moving.

Capability focus

Six disciplines that make you worth hiring

The curriculum develops the exact commercial behaviours companies need from the people they trust with revenue.

No. 01

Sales communication

Speak clearly, listen properly and guide a conversation without ever sounding scripted.

No. 02

Discovery & qualification

Learn whether there's a real fit before you try to move anyone anywhere.

No. 03

Objection handling

Stay calm when resistance shows up, and answer with structure instead of pressure.

No. 04

Closing frameworks

Guide strong conversations to a clear next step without forcing a thing.

No. 05

Professional development

Reliability, presence, communication and daily output. The quiet standards that compound.

No. 06

Accountability

Track progress, take feedback and keep moving on the days motivation doesn't show up.

Online, never alone

A rhythm you can feel

Over two hundred live sessions a month keep you close to feedback. Peers keep ambition normal. Mentors keep the bar where it belongs. Progress stops depending on willpower because the environment carries you on the days you'd usually drift.
