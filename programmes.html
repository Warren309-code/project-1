Programmes

Pathways built for the digital economy

Not more theory. Each programme is a working route from where you are to where the market actually hires, with coaching and community holding the standard the whole way.

Core pathway

Digital Sales Programme

Remote sales is the fastest honest route into the digital economy, and the skill that keeps paying wherever you take it. Structured training, live coaching and placement readiness, built around communication, confidence and discipline.

  • Communication, discovery, objection handling and closing frameworks
  • Mock calls, live call reviews, one to one coaching, group workshops
  • Placement support once you can show discipline and progress
Affinity for Women

Remote careers, on your terms

A practical route into customer success, executive support, operations and flexible digital work. Built around confidence, real support and clearer entry points, inside a community that understands what independence is worth.

  • Customer success, EA, support and operations pathways
  • Tailored one to one sessions and a supportive room to practise in
  • Designed for flexibility, momentum and financial independence
Graduate Accelerator

The bridge after education

For graduates who did everything right and still feel underprepared for the real hiring market. A faster, more practical transition from study to work, built on confidence, communication and professional standards.

  • Career clarity and digital work readiness
  • Interview confidence and everyday professional communication
  • A stronger entry into modern, remote-first hiring markets
Career Reset

For roles that no longer fit

You've outgrown the job, not the ambition. A structured path to rebuild direction, skills and earning potential in the digital economy. Practical movement, honest coaching, and a plan that respects the life you've already built.

  • Ideal when the current path has stalled or stopped fitting
  • Focused on measurable progress, not vague motivation
  • Built to move you into better-fit, better-paid work
Why sales first

The skill that opens every door

Clear communication, listening, resilience and the nerve to own an outcome. Sales trains the behaviours every digital career is built on, which is why our graduates move so easily into customer success, operations, recruiting and beyond.
