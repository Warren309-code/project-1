Programmes

Courses built around real outcomes

Each Affinity course is more than a content library. Students move through structured modules, live calls, personal tutor support, AI training, practical assessments, credits and career-readiness checkpoints before progressing toward opportunities.

Courses

Choose the pathway that matches the next move.

Digital Sales Programme

Remote sales is the fastest honest route into the digital economy, and the skill that keeps paying wherever you take it. Structured training, live coaching and placement readiness, built around communication, confidence and discipline.

  • Communication, discovery, objection handling and closing frameworks
  • Mock calls, live call reviews, one to one coaching, group workshops
  • Placement support once you can show discipline and progress

Affinity for Women

A practical route into customer success, executive support, operations and flexible digital work. Built around confidence, real support and clearer entry points, inside a community that understands what independence is worth.

  • Customer success, EA, support and operations pathways
  • Tailored one to one sessions and a supportive room to practise in
  • Designed for flexibility, momentum and financial independence

Digital Tech Sales Programme

For people aiming at larger enterprise and B2B sales environments. The same coached foundation as the core pathway, tuned for longer deal cycles, multiple stakeholders, technical buyers and the standards expected of remote B2B teams.

  • Enterprise deal cycles, stakeholder mapping and consultative selling
  • Tech literacy, demos and discovery for technical buyers
  • Placement readiness for B2B SDR, AE and partnership roles

AI Programme

Learn to use AI to improve real business processes and solve practical problems. Identify where AI can add value, design and build AI-enabled workflows, and make sure they work reliably and safely — without needing a coding background.

  • Spot where AI adds value and design reliable, safe AI-enabled workflows
  • Hands-on projects in AI operations, problem-solving, communication, testing and delivery
  • A verified project and portfolio for employment, freelance work or your own AI-enabled business
Why sales first

The skill that opens every door

Clear communication, listening, resilience and the nerve to own an outcome. Sales trains the behaviours every digital career is built on, which is why our graduates move so easily into customer success, operations, recruiting and beyond.
